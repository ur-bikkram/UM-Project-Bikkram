# Executive Summary: EduPro Learner Demographics & Enrollment Behavior

**Prepared for:** Platform Leadership and Stakeholders  
**Date:** September 2026  
**Scope:** Analysis of 10,000 enrollments across 3,000 learners and 60 courses (2025 data)  
**Document Identifier:** EDUPRO-ES-2026-09  

---

## Overview

This summary presents the core findings from an analysis of learner registration and enrollment records on EduPro during 2025. The goal was to provide platform leadership with clear, objective facts about who uses the platform and how they select courses, replacing intuition with data-backed findings.

## Key Findings

### 1. Gender Equity Across Technical Fields
Female and male participation is evenly balanced across the platform, with 50.8% female enrollments and 49.2% male enrollments. In technical subjects such as Data Science (50.8% female) and Artificial Intelligence (50.6% female), representation is equal. Both groups also enroll in free and paid courses at identical rates (64.0% free, 36.0% paid).

### 2. High Demand for Data Science, Finance, and Web Development
Course categories do not perform equally. Using the Category Popularity Index (where 100 represents the average demand per course):
- **Data Science** (CPI: 109.9; 916 enrollments)
- **Finance** (CPI: 103.7; 864 enrollments)
- **Web Development** (CPI: 101.3; 844 enrollments)
- **Business** (CPI: 100.0; 833 enrollments)

These domains lead in enrollment density per course, indicating where learner demand is strongest.

### 3. Engagement Intensity Is Constant, but Drop-Off Is High
Learners in all age groups take roughly the same number of courses on average (3.39 courses per user for under 18, 3.33 for ages 18–25, and 3.32 for ages 26–35). However, **54.0% of all users have taken only a single course**. Meanwhile, a dedicated group of power users (the top 20%) accounts for 66.5% of all enrollments.

---

## Recommended Next Steps

1. **Invest in Data Science, Finance, and Web Development:**  
   Prioritize new course production in Data Science, Finance, and Web Development to meet demonstrated demand, expanding from 5 to 8–10 offerings per category.

2. **Differentiate Outreach by Age:**  
   - Market web development and design to secondary school learners (<18).  
   - Market career certifications in technical fields and project management to learners aged 18 to 35.  
   - Consider introducing professional certificates to attract adult learners over 35, who are currently unrepresented.

3. **Focus on the Second Course:**  
   Because 54.0% of learners stop after one course, implement automated next-course recommendations and structured learning tracks (e.g. "Data Analyst Track") to encourage single-course learners to continue their studies.
